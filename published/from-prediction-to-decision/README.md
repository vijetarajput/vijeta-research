# From Prediction to Decision: An Operational Audit Framework for AI Driven Lending, Based on Traditional Banking Controls

Preprint submitted to SSRN, July 22, 2026.
SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=7161058 (approval pending, going live shortly)

The paper translates four traditional banking controls, sanctioning authority
chains, red flag checklists, early warning monitoring and maker checker review,
into practical operational controls for AI lending systems, with India as the
context. Based on my experience managing 2,500 to 3,000 loan accounts and
reporting across 27 branches in a regional rural bank.

The framework comes with four open templates: a decision ownership register,
a one page guardrail policy, an outcome monitoring dashboard structure, and a
review queue log. These will be published in the `toolkit` folder of this repo.

This paper extends my earlier work on decision centric analytics:
https://ssrn.com/abstract=6667840
