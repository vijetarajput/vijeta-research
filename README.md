# Vijeta Research

Hi, I'm Vijeta. I spent 10+ years in Indian banking and fintech, managing rural loan portfolios, building risk dashboards, and later building a SaaS product with KYC and payment workflows. I hold an MSc in Blockchain and Financial Technologies from the University of East London.

This repo is my public research desk. I research how data, AI and blockchain systems are used in finance, and how they can be kept accountable, auditable, verifiable and explainable, especially in markets like India and the UK.

## Papers

**1. Designing Decision-Centric Banking Analytics Systems: A Practical Framework from Regional Banking Environments** (April 2026)
SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=6667840 · DOI: https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6667840

A practical framework for building analytics systems that actually influence decisions, based on a real implementation across 27 banking branches. 40% reduction in reporting time, 15% improvement in NPA recovery.

**2. From Prediction to Decision: An Operational Audit Framework for AI Driven Lending, Based on Traditional Banking Controls** (August 2026)
SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=7161058 · DOI: https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7161058

Translates four traditional banking controls, sanctioning authority chains, red flag checklists, early warning monitoring and maker checker review, into practical operational controls for AI lending systems, with India as the context. Extends Paper 1's core finding: systems only work when they enter daily decision making.

**3. Sterling Stablecoins On-Chain: A Multi-Source Verification Study of Market Structure, Regulatory Models and Payment Use** (October 2026, preprint, under review)
SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=7627799 · DOI: https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7627799 · Data and code: https://github.com/vijetarajput/gbp-stablecoin-analysis

Verifies six British pound stablecoins across five blockchains and finds a very small, highly concentrated market with four distinct issuance models and uneven consumer protection. A twelve-month analysis of more than 84,000 transfers shows that 78% of one token's transactions were card payments made by a small, recurring and declining group of users. Extends the verification theme of Paper 2 from AI systems to on-chain financial data.

## Policy engagement

**Feedback to the Reserve Bank of India on the Draft Guidance on Regulatory Principles for Model Risk Management, 2026** (July 2026)
Submitted via RBI's Connect 2 Regulate portal, Public Consultation No. C2R/2026-27/487. Drawing on Regional Rural Bank experience and Paper 2, it recommends simple operational artefacts for smaller regulated entities: named decision owners in the model inventory, outcome monitoring by model version and borrower segment, and a maker checker style human review tier for high impact decisions.
Full text: [policy-engagement/rbi-model-risk-comment-2026.md](policy-engagement/rbi-model-risk-comment-2026.md)
