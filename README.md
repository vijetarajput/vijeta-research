# Vijeta Research

Hi, I'm Vijeta. I spent 10+ years in Indian banking and fintech, managing rural
loan portfolios, building risk dashboards, and later building a SaaS product
with KYC and payment workflows.

This repo is my public research desk. I research how data and AI systems take
high stakes decisions in finance, and how those decisions can be kept
accountable, auditable and explainable, especially in markets like India.

## Papers

**1. Designing Decision-Centric Banking Analytics Systems: A Practical
Framework from Regional Banking Environments** (April 2026)
SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=6667840
DOI: http://dx.doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6667840

A practical framework for building analytics systems that actually influence
decisions, based on a real implementation across 27 banking branches. 40%
reduction in reporting time, 15% improvement in NPA recovery.

**2. From Prediction to Decision: An Operational Audit Framework for AI
Driven Lending, Based on Traditional Banking Controls** (July 2026)
SSRN: https://ssrn.com/abstract=7161058 (approval pending, going live shortly)

Translates four traditional banking controls, sanctioning authority chains,
red flag checklists, early warning monitoring and maker checker review, into
practical operational controls for AI lending systems, with India as the
context. Extends Paper 1's core finding: systems only work when they enter
daily decision making.
